Questions people actually ask

What happens on the free call?

Thirty minutes with someone from the Balance Bae team. You talk about where things stand: the business, your head, or both. At the end you get a straight recommendation, including "you don't need us right now" if that's the honest answer. Nothing to prepare, no pitch at the end.

Who is this for?

Women in their twenties and thirties building a business, freelancing, or pushing a career forward without much of a structure around them. You don't need to be in crisis, and you don't need a funded startup. You just need to be doing something hard on your own.

What does Career Growth actually include?

Two 1:1 sessions a month with Janvi, worksheets and exercises built around your specific situation, help with how you present yourself professionally and publicly, and async chat for the questions that come up between sessions.

Who provides the mental support, and are they certified?

Licensed, certified therapists. Not coaches, not peer listeners. You're matched with one based on what you're dealing with and how you like to work. If the match isn't right, you can switch.

Is this therapy?

The Mental Support track is real therapy with a certified therapist. The Career Growth track is not therapy. It's professional guidance. They're deliberately kept distinct, and your sessions with your therapist are confidential.

Can I take both?

Yes. That's the Both membership. Career and mental support run in parallel, with priority scheduling and a monthly check-in to reassess what you need more of.

How does the monthly membership work?

Monthly, billed each month, cancel whenever you want. You can move between packages at any point. Plenty of people start with one track and add the other later.

What if I don't know what I need?

Then the free call is exactly the right place to start. Most people book it precisely because they can't tell whether it's a business problem or a them problem. Usually it's some of each.

You don't need to have it figured out to start.
